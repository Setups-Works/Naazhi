About the Brand

Naazhi is one workspace for the days, work and thoughts of one person.

Notes, to-dos, journal, chat, calendar and a Reader live together, on the web and on the phone. An AI layer can help, and it can be swapped for another provider, replaced with your own key, or switched off.

The product is calm on purpose. It uses clean Geist typography, high-contrast ink, crisp hairline borders and flat paper surfaces, and it says what happened and what to do next.

Notes, tasks, journal

The things you write and plan.

Chat with an optional AI

Swap it, bring your own, or turn it off.

Calendar and Reader

Your day and what you follow.

Private by design

Row-level security, private storage.

Brand Story

நாழி

An old Tamil measure of grain, and of time. A small, honest unit you use every day.

The name Naazhi (நாழி, said NAA-zhee) comes from a measure people once used to portion out both grain and time: small, plain, trusted, daily.

That is the idea behind the product. Our days are scattered across a dozen apps, and each one asks for attention. Naazhi keeps the days, the work and the thoughts of one person in one place, and keeps the loud parts quiet.

The mark is drawn the way Tamil is written: one hand-drawn line with rounded ends that curls, loops and closes into a speech bubble. It was made to feel personal, not corporate.

022

Logo Construction

1 · Curl2 · Loop 3 · Star4 · Bubble + lines
  1. Curl. The small hook at the top, where the stroke begins.
  2. Loop. The round counter in the middle, the heart of the shape.
  3. Star. A four-point spark at about 40 % of the green. Never recolour it separately.
  4. Bubble with three lines. The long tail closes into a speech bubble holding three lines of writing.

The mark is a hand-drawn master picture, processed into transparent PNGs by scripts/brand/build-assets.mjs. Don’t redraw it from memory.

045

Typography Do's & Don'ts

Do

  • Use Geist for all headings, body text, buttons and controls.
  • Reserve Instrument Serif strictly for the Naazhi wordmark.
  • Write in sentence case with curly apostrophes (’).
  • Keep line length near 60–75 characters.

Don’t

  • Use Instrument Serif for page titles, buttons or body text.
  • Use Title Case Buttons or ALL CAPS headings.
  • Go below 11 px or use faint text for body copy.
  • Add bold or italic to Instrument Serif (it has one weight).

Brand Patterns

Patterns are quiet backgrounds built from the brand’s own forms. They sit at low opacity behind content and never compete with it.

  • Marks: the mark repeated on a diagonal grid at 6 % opacity.
  • Lines: the three writing lines, repeated.
  • Dots: a dot grid on paper for planning surfaces.
